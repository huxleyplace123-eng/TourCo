Questions people actually ask

Before you call.

Does switching mid-policy cost me anything?
Almost never on auto — Arizona carriers refund unearned premium pro-rata, so you get the unused months back. Some home policies carry a short-rate penalty; we check before you commit and tell you the number.
Will you pull my credit?
Carriers use an insurance score, which is a soft pull. It doesn't affect your credit score, and we don't run anything until you've seen a price you like.
I need an SR-22 today. Can you do it?
Yes. We file electronically with MVD, usually within the hour during business hours. Bring your citation number and the case disposition if you have it.
My roof is 18 years old. Am I uninsurable?
No, but your market shrinks and you'll likely be offered actual-cash-value roof coverage instead of replacement cost. We'll show you both prices so you can decide whether a re-roof pays for itself.
What does it cost to work with you?
Nothing. We're paid a commission by whichever carrier you choose, at the same rate across carriers — so there's no version of this where we make more by putting you somewhere worse.
